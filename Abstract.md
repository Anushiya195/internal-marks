# Project Abstract

## Internal Marks Management System

The **Internal Marks Management System (IMMS)** is a web-based application designed to streamline the process of managing and calculating internal assessment marks for educational institutions. In many colleges, the manual calculation of Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) marks, assignments, and lab records is time-consuming and prone to errors. This system provides a centralized platform for administrators, faculty, and students to efficiently handle academic records.

The system automates the calculation of total marks and grades based on predefined criteria, ensuring accuracy and transparency. It allows the **Administrator** to manage the institutional data (departments, subjects, users), **Faculty** to input and update student performance data, and **Students** to view their academic progress in real-time. By moving this process online, the IMMS reduces paperwork, improves data accessibility, and enhances the overall efficiency of academic administration.
